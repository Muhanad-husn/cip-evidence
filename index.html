Conflict Intelligence Platform

A conflict, measured end to end.

CIP reads reporting on a conflict in Arabic, Persian, Hebrew and English, builds one record of events, actors, claims and relationships, and tests that record against measured behaviour such as strait transits, air traffic and economic indicators. Every result is dated, reproducible and carries its confidence level. The record on this site covers the Iran war from 28 February to 14 November 2026.

The proof

The platform ships a product every week.

Every week the chain collects, processes, analyses and writes an edition without an operator at the controls.

The editions are published as Conflict Gauge: Iran, a separate MergeSeat product sold by subscription. A weekly edition appears every Monday at 06:00 Amsterdam time, a monthly edition follows each month, and the archive reaches back to the first day of the war.

The latest weekly cycle, for 8 to 14 November 2026, ran from start to finish in 32 minutes and cost USD 4.94 in model and data-query spend. The founder reviews each edition before it is published.
