Deployment and integration

Installed where the organisation decides.

CIP is a platform an organisation runs for its own analysts or for its clients. The conflict, the languages, the sources and the areas of interest are settings of the platform, not a rebuild.

Discuss a deployment The partner route

Inputs

Public reporting, primary sources and the organisation's own collection.

CIP works on open and primary sources alike. It reads reporting in Arabic, Persian, Hebrew and English, and it reads measured series such as strait transits, air traffic and economic indicators beside the reporting.

The organisation's own collection is a first-class input. Incident logs and field reporting from its own staff are taken in beside the public reporting and placed on one timeline, under the same checks.

Adaptation

A new source in days, a new area in a week.

About 2 days

A new data source

A feed, a channel or a structured dataset is added as a source with its own reliability tier.

About 1 week

A new area of interest

A new theatre brings its places, its actors and its measured series.

A setting

A new language

A further language is a change of settings, because all analysis runs on one translated record.

Delivery

Briefs, tables and data files other systems read directly.

  • Written briefs in PDF, weekly and monthly, with a confidence level on every finding.
  • A spreadsheet-ready table behind every figure.
  • Data files that other systems read directly.
  • Map and graph views for analysts who work in the platform itself.

The weekly editions of Conflict Gauge are this delivery in production.

Installation

The data stays where the organisation keeps it.

In the organisation's cloud tenancy

Installed in an account the organisation controls.

On a server on its premises

Installed on hardware the organisation owns.

In a sealed environment

Installed with no outbound connection, for collections that may not leave the building.

A hosted tenant with a direct interface is built on request. Language-model use runs on the organisation's own provider account, so the organisation chooses the provider and sees the spend.

Partners

Delivered under the partner's name.

Intelligence providers and risk consultancies run CIP for their own analysts and clients. Results are delivered under the partner's name, on terms agreed with each partner.

The entry step

The first step is a paid scoping on one live question, on the organisation's own sources, with the result delivered as a brief. Its fee is credited against a licence.
