Founder

Muhanad AbulHusn

An engineer who delivered under constraint on landmark projects, and an analyst who served the institutions that buy conflict intelligence. CIP is where the two meet.

Muhanad AbulHusn is an AI implementation specialist with ten years in social data science on conflict and armed groups and in OSINT. He is the founder of MergeSeat and the sole developer of CIP.

His conflict work has been with the University of Dublin, CIR in the United Kingdom, Bellingcat in the United Kingdom and the Netherlands, and the Atlantic Council in Washington. Since mid-2024 he has been an on-demand consultant to the Dutch foreign ministry and the United States Department of State on armed groups and group dynamics.

He trained in engineering and business administration and spent five years in construction in the United Arab Emirates, from 2005 to 2010, working on Burj Khalifa and on Dubai Airport Terminal 3, where Dubai Civil Aviation recognised him for solving a firefighting problem on site. He rose from quality inspector to construction manager in those five years.

CIP is that expertise automated. It is designed against seven analyst personas, each of them a professional community he has worked in directly. Before CIP he built Neo4All and G-Lab, which turn document collections into governed knowledge graphs for investigative work, and SocioRAG and SpecClass for retrieval and document classification. CIP is the fourth system of its kind he has taken from schema to running code.
